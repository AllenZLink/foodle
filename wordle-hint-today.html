Tips for tougher days

  • Start with vowel-rich words. ADIEU, OUIJA, AUDIO each cover four vowels in a single guess.
  • Burn a second guess on consonants. If your opener was vowel-heavy, follow up with something like CRYPT or NYMPH to rule out clusters.
  • Mind double letters. Wordle reuses common pairs — LL, EE, OO, SS — so don't rule them out after one yellow.
  • Endings carry information. If you have a green letter at position 5, try common endings: -OUND, -IGHT, -ATCH.

Why does the NYT change the puzzle list?

The Times curates Wordle daily, occasionally swapping out words for editorial reasons — sensitivity, current events, or simple variety. So an old solver list won't always match. We update this page once per morning Eastern time so the hints stay aligned with what's actually live.
