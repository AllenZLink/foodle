Welcome to Foodle Daily — the slow, savory cousin of your morning coffee ritual. Each midnight (your local time) the kitchen swaps in a new five-, six-, or seven-letter food word: an ingredient, a dish, a technique, a snack from somewhere on the map. You get six tries on the five-letter board, seven on the six, and eight on the seven. Solve it, and we'll tell you a quick story about what you found.

How to play

Type any real word of the right length and hit Enter. Each guess fills the row, then flips to show how close you got:

  • Green — the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
  • Yellow — the letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
  • Gray — the letter isn't in the word at all.

Use early guesses to test vowels and common consonants. Save your last few rows for hard commits. If you're stuck, the 💡 Hint button gives you a category nudge, then a starting letter, then a definition — but each hint costs you one of your remaining guesses, so spend them carefully.

Tips for the daily board

  1. Open with a vowel-heavy word. ADIEU, AUDIO, OUIJA all spend four of five tiles on vowels — you'll know fast which way to lean.
  2. Don't burn green letters. If your second row already has a confirmed green, keep it in place even if you're tempted to swap.
  3. Watch for repeated letters. Foodle words like CHEESE, COFFEE, BANANA all repeat — a yellow doesn't mean "this letter only appears once."
  4. Switch lengths if you're stumped. The 6- and 7-letter boards have entirely different word pools, and sometimes the longer board is actually easier because there's more signal per guess.

Frequently asked questions

What time does the daily word change?

At local midnight, in your time zone. We pick a deterministic word per calendar day, so your friend in Tokyo and you in Los Angeles are solving the same puzzle on the same date.

Can I play yesterday's puzzle?

Not on the daily board — the whole point is that today is today. If you want unlimited replays, hop over to Unlimited mode. If you want a fresh puzzle every hour, try Hourly.

Are the words always English?

Yes — common-English food words from around the world. Loanwords like RAMEN, KIMCHI, GELATO and MATCHA are fair game because they live in everyday English now.

Why did my streak reset?

The daily streak counts consecutive solved days. If you skip a day or fail to solve, it falls back to zero — but your "best streak" sticks around as a personal record.

Is there a hint penalty?

Each hint you reveal costs one of your remaining guesses. Three hints are available per puzzle. The cost is deliberate — hints should feel like a real trade.

Where are my stats stored?

Right on your device, in your browser's local storage. We don't have user accounts, and your data never leaves your machine. See our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

I think today's word is unfair. What now?

Take a breath, eat something, write us a strongly worded but ultimately forgiving email at hello@foodle.game. We read everything.

Foodle is its own thing, but if you're here because you're stuck on today's New York Times Wordle, we keep a separate hint page that's updated every morning: today's NYT Wordle hint & answer.
