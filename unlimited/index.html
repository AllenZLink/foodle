How unlimited differs

  • No streak. Streaks reward consistency — unlimited rewards volume. The two would fight, so we let unlimited be its own thing.
  • No timer. Take five seconds or five hours per word. The countdown chip is hidden on this mode.
  • "Next word" is the main button. Win or lose, it's right there at the bottom of the food card. Tap, get a new word, keep going.
  • Stats are tracked separately. Your unlimited record (played, won, win %) is its own column.

How to get the most out of it

  1. Treat the first 10 rounds as warmup. Use them to test openers and learn the food-word distribution.
  2. Cycle through the lengths. Each board (5, 6, 7) has a different feel — five is fast, six rewards anagram instinct, seven punishes overcommitting early.
  3. Don't chase win rate. Unlimited mode is the place to take wild guesses without paying for them. Your daily streak is safe over here.
  4. Use hints freely. They're meant to be used. The cost feels lower when there's another puzzle waiting one tap away.

Unlimited FAQ

Why isn't there an unlimited streak?

Streaks measure scarcity — one chance per day, did you take it? Unlimited has the opposite shape. Every loss is a tap away from a fresh round, so a streak there would be all noise and no signal.

Will I see the same word twice?

Eventually, yes. The food word pool is curated, not infinite. We don't actively dedupe within a session, so a long Sunday afternoon will turn up some repeats. Treat them as a chance to set a personal speed record.

Does unlimited share words with daily and hourly?

Yes — same curated pool. The difference is the seed: daily picks one word per calendar day, hourly picks one per clock hour, and unlimited picks at random each round.

Can I share an unlimited result?

You can — every solved game generates the same color-block grid you'd share from daily. We just don't tag it with a puzzle number, since unlimited rounds aren't synchronized.

Will unlimited stats sync to my daily?

No. They're tracked in their own bucket, on your device. The four numbers (played, win %, current streak, max streak) you see on the stats screen reflect the mode you're currently playing.

Is unlimited a good mode for kids learning English?

Surprisingly so. The food vocabulary is concrete, the win cards explain origins, and there's no time pressure. Hints are forgiving. We've heard from a few families using it as a low-stakes nightly word study.
